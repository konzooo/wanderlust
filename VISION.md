# Wanderlust — Vision

> Living document. Captures the founding vision, principles, positioning, and language for Wanderlust (codebase name: TravelBuddy). Reference this for tone, concept, and product decisions.

## The core idea

People travel a lot, and often before trips they look for inspiration. They ask friends, they read blogs, they go on Instagram looking at videos, and nowadays they also ask ChatGPT.

**We want to be another source of travel inspiration.**

## What we are NOT

- **Not a planning app.** A planning app will not stick — in the end people prefer many sources themselves and don't want to get stuck with one app.
- **Not a productivity app.** We should be a fun, modern, and easy way to get inspired.

## The twist

Even though we're not a productivity app, we actually *also* deliver more productivity, because:

- We **save** the recommendations for you.
- We have **direct links to maps**, etc.
- We give **small itineraries** (and more features in the future).

**Crucially:** these features and itineraries are a **means to an end, not the end in themselves.** It's not about structuring the day for the user — it's about having a **common way to display suggestions**.

Why this matters: all the other methods (friends, blogs, Instagram, ChatGPT) let you *read* but don't *save* info for you specifically. And a ChatGPT chat is interesting, but the information is very unstructured.

## The key competitive challenge: competing with ChatGPT

The key part, above all, is nowadays to compete with ChatGPT — and this is a true ongoing challenge, since we are ourselves based on LLM layers.

So we need to do at least one of:

1. **Add additional value.**
2. **Display LLM information better.**
3. **Make it better** through better context and less need for the user to type endless context themselves.

That last point is one of our key principles.

## Key principle: personality-based recommendations

Where we score right now is that we have a **structured way to get your preferences**, so we can give you **non-generic, good recommendations.**

**Tagline / mental image:** *Imagine travelling with a friend who knows you well, but also happens to be a local wherever you go.*

How we do it today: we apply frameworks from psychology's personality tests (like the **Big Five**) and build an analogous concept for **understanding travel preferences.**

In the future, the app can **learn more about you the more you use it.**

## Positioning summary

- **Feel:** fun, modern, easy — a source of *inspiration*, not a chore.
- **Value over ChatGPT:** structured preferences → non-generic recommendations; saved for you; direct links (maps); structured display of otherwise-unstructured LLM output; less context-typing burden on the user.
- **Vision line:** a friend who knows you well *and* is a local everywhere you go.
