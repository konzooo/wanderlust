Pin + plane, done properly

Back to what people already recognise. The pin and the aeroplane are drawn once, as real geometry — a circular head with a true tangent taper, an airliner with swept wings and a tailplane — and reused in all ten. That is the difference between this and the icon you have: same idea, drawn instead of assembled.

The spark survives where it earns its place, at about a tenth of the mark. Loose mark, app icon, then 44 / 28 / 18px and reversed on ink.

Read this as a ladder

01 is your current logo with the drawing fixed — true tangents, a real aircraft, correct optical weight. If nothing else here wins, ship this: it is the same brand, minus the cheapness.

02 is 01 plus your spark, at the ratio you asked for: travel first, a tenth of AI second. That is the safest place to land.

03 Cut and 04 Fusion are the two that would actually look like a 2026 brand. Cut makes the icon do the work with two levels of negative space; Fusion is the only one where pin and plane are a single shape rather than one inside the other — the most ownable thing on the sheet.

07, 08 are richest and weakest small. 05 I'd avoid: plane-only marks are the most crowded space in travel.
