## Audience and positioning

Wanderlust is for leisure travellers who want travel inspiration to feel fun,
modern, easy, and personal rather than like productivity work. Its core
advantage is turning structured travel preferences into non-generic
recommendations without requiring users to write extensive prompts or assemble
information scattered across friends, blogs, social media, maps, and chats.

The app is positioned as a well-travelled friend who knows the user personally
and is also a local everywhere they go. Saved recommendations, map links,
itineraries, favourites, and group trips support that inspiration experience.
